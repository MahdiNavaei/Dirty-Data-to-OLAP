# Dirty Data to OLAP — Reference Benchmark Glossary

Scope: **REFERENCE BENCHMARK DOMAIN TRUTH only**. These definitions are not universal product semantics.

| Canonical term | Reference-domain definition | What it is not / common confusion | Identity and temporal notes | Source representations | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer | A real-world buyer/person or organization represented in the synthetic customer population. | Not every row in a table named `customers`, `clients` or `old_customers`. | Same customer may have several source records; hidden benchmark membership is generated separately. Snapshot semantics; full history unresolved. | CRM `customers`; ERP `clients_legacy`; `old_customers.csv`. | Ground truth |
| Customer Address | A postal/contact-address representation associated with a Customer in the benchmark snapshot. | Not a Customer identity and not proof that two customers are the same. | Source-record identity is separate from customer identity; address history is not defined. | CRM `customer_addresses`; possible customer-related legacy representation. | Ground truth, history unresolved |
| Campaign Membership | A record that associates a Customer representation with a campaign in the CRM scenario. | Not an order, payment or proof of customer identity. | Membership event timing and campaign lifecycle codes are generator-defined later. | CRM `campaign_memberships`. | Ground truth concept; code mapping unresolved |
| Product | A sellable product concept in the benchmark product master. | Not a product alias string or an order-line occurrence. | Canonical identity is stable within the generated benchmark; source codes may differ. | ERP `products`; `product_aliases.csv`. | Ground truth |
| Product Alias | A legacy or alternate representation that may map to a Product. | Not an independent Product merely because its text is similar. | Alias-to-product mapping is benchmark-labelled later; description equality alone is insufficient. | `product_aliases.csv`. | Ground truth concept; row mapping later |
| Branch | A physical or organizational selling location to which an Order may be attributed. | Not a generic integer code domain or a customer address. | One valid Order has one benchmark branch attribution; physical corruption may remove or orphan it. | ERP `branches`; `branch_codes.parquet`. | Ground truth |
| Order | A commercial order-header event created in the Sales system. | Not an OrderLine, payment event or recognized-revenue record. | Canonical identity is the order event; valid order has one Customer and one Branch in this benchmark. | Sales `orders`. | Ground truth |
| Order Line / OrderLine | A product-level line event within one Order, representing one product and its ordered quantity/price context. | Not the Order header and not a duplicate merely because another line shares its order. | Intended business grain is Order identity plus line sequence; exact physical field spelling is generator-defined. | Sales `order_items`. | Ground truth |
| Payment | A payment event associated with an Order. | Not proof that revenue was recognized or that an Order has exactly one payment. | Each event has one Order; an Order may have zero or more Payment events. Exact event-key columns are generator-defined. | Sales `payments`. | Ground truth; code/status details unresolved |
| Date / Calendar Date | A calendar date derived from benchmark event dates for analytical grouping. | Not a time-zone, settlement or accounting calendar policy. | `dim_date` is derived; locale-specific and fiscal-calendar semantics are outside current scope. | Derived from Sales event dates. | Ground truth concept; extended calendars unresolved |
| CRM Customer Record | A source record containing a CRM representation of a Customer profile and/or address. | Not the canonical Customer itself and not universal authority for all attributes. | CRM record identity is source-scoped; current profile authority expectations are attribute-scoped. | PostgreSQL `customers`, `customer_addresses`, `campaign_memberships`. | Ground truth representation |
| ERP Legacy Client Record | A historical or legacy ERP representation that may correspond to a Customer. | Not proof that `client_no` equals a CRM identifier or that ERP owns current contact values. | Linkage uses benchmark truth plus evidence; legacy temporal coverage is limited. | MySQL `clients_legacy`. | Ground truth representation |
| Legacy Customer Record | A file-based historical customer representation used to exercise cross-source linkage. | Not automatically a new Customer and not current profile authority. | May be stale, partial or duplicated by design; exact row-level cluster labels come later. | `old_customers.csv`. | Ground truth representation |
