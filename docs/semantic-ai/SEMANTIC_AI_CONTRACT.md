# Semantic AI Contract

Step16 exposes optional, candidate-only semantic evidence through project-owned Pydantic contracts. The only V1 tasks are column business meaning, relationship semantic hypothesis, table-role hypothesis and ambiguity explanation. SQL generation, cleaning, joins, acceptance, repair and general chat are outside the boundary.

The provider returns support, contradiction, ambiguity or insufficient-context states with bounded rationales and evidence references. It never returns numeric confidence, canonical truth or mutation authority. Evidence Fusion remains a later Step17 responsibility.
