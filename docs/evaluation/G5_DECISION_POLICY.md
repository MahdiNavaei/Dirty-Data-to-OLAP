# G5 Inference Validity Decision Policy

G5 may be formally `PASS` with a separate `inference_validity_mode=REVIEW_ONLY_VALIDATED` only when the frozen protocol is bound before TEST, groups are disjoint, every required provider output is normalized and loaded for the claimed task, truth is isolated from runtime inference, task metrics and denominators are present, artifact hashes verify, and calibration/threshold controls remain non-authorizing.

The historical Step18 v1 report claimed `REVIEW_ONLY_VALIDATED` from real provider receipts, but its headline metrics were not bound to those outputs. The v4 continuation supersedes that quality claim: Desbordante, Profiling, and Quality outputs are consumed by the evaluator; Valentine and Splink remain unavailable; relationship semantic reproducibility now passes across clean equivalent inputs, with exact byte differences retained as a separate integrity fact. Formal G5 remains `PENDING` because required schema/entity tasks are incomplete. Automation remains disabled and the assessment explicitly says `NOT_AUTHORIZED`. Optional semantic AI was not executed. This status is not production, release, acceptance, canonical identity, or repair approval.

Offline runtime availability alone does not satisfy the policy. In the current continuation, Valentine is executable but Cupid is incomplete due absent offline NLTK corpora, while Splink is executable but does not reach fully trained model state on the frozen population. Both cases remain insufficient evidence; no default parameters, handcrafted scores, or labels may upgrade them. Schema producer contracts are complete but cannot mask matcher incompleteness. G5 therefore remains `PENDING` / `UNVALIDATED`, with no selected automation threshold and no Step19 transition.
