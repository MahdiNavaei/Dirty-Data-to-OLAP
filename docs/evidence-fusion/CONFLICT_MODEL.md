# Conflict Model

The first-class conflict types are `SEMANTIC_STRUCTURAL_CONFLICT`,
`DECLARED_DATA_CONFLICT`, `TYPE_SEMANTIC_CONFLICT`,
`SAMPLE_FULLSCAN_CONFLICT` and `MULTIPLE_TARGET_AMBIGUITY`.

Conflicts retain supporting and contradicting evidence, scope and a
deterministic explanation. A conflict forces the operational band to
`CONFLICTED` and the decision to remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, regardless of its
diagnostic score.
