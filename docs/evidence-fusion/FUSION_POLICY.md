# Fusion Policy

The committed policies are `relationship-fusion-v1` and `mapping-fusion-v1`.
Both are version `1.0`, `UNCALIBRATED`, automation-disabled and require G5
before any future promotion. Bounded [0,1] structural metrics use an explicit
identity transform; matcher evidence uses matcher-specific ordinal rank;
LLM and learned evidence are qualitative/derived by default.

Policy content is hashed and included in the request and decision identity.
