# Scoring and Confidence

The score is a transparent, versioned ranking diagnostic. It records eligible
and observed contribution weight, coverage, per-signal contributions and
`confidence_kind=UNCALIBRATED_SCORE`. Structural metrics retain their original
semantics. COMA, Cupid, Splink and learned scores are not averaged as if they
were equivalent quantities.

Operational bands are not calibrated probabilities. Step18 owns evaluation,
calibration and any later policy promotion.
