# Missing and Scope Semantics

Missing, skipped, unavailable, privacy-blocked, failed and incomplete producer
states are distinct from a measured zero. Required Profile, Dependency and
Quality results must be complete. Cross-source mapping additionally requires a
complete Schema Matching result. Optional ML/LLM absence is recorded and does
not become negative evidence.

Signals preserve FULL, BOUNDED, SAMPLED, NULL_REDUCED, TEMPORALLY_UNALIGNED or
INCOMPLETE scope/reliability states. Incompatible snapshots fail the subject
scope check; timestamps are not used in deterministic decision IDs.
